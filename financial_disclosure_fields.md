# Financial Disclosure Form — Field Mapping

## Section 1: To be completed by education staff (pre-filled by admin)
- Name of Individual (faculty name)
- Title of Continuing Education (course title)
- Date and location of Education

## Section 2: Individual's prospective role(s) in education (checkboxes — faculty selects)
- [ ] Planner (Examples: planning committee, staff involved in choosing topics, faculty, or content)
- [ ] Teacher, Instructor, Faculty
- [ ] Author, Writer
- [ ] Reviewer
- [ ] Other (free text)

## Section 3: Financial Relationships Table (repeating rows, up to ~7)
Each row has:
- Company Name (ineligible company)
- Nature of Financial Relationship (e.g., employee, researcher, consultant, advisor, speaker, etc.)
- Has the Relationship Ended? (checkbox)

## Section 4: No relationships checkbox
- [ ] In the past 24 months, I have not had any financial relationships with any ineligible companies.

## Section 5: Attestation
- "I attest that the above information is correct as of this date of submission."
- (Signature area — electronic signature or typed name + date)
