Important: Cohort courses (live sessions, discussions, and replays) are accessed on the Learning Platform at learn.allaboutultrasound.com. Use this site — not the clinical app — for your cohort activities.

1. Getting started — accessing the learning site

Sign in

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge recommended).
  2. Go to the sign-in page:
https://learn.allaboutultrasound.com/login
  1. Sign in using one of these methods:
    • Password: Enter your email and password, then click Sign in with password.
    • Magic link: Choose Send Magic Link, enter your email, then click the link sent to your inbox (valid for 15 minutes).
  2. If you are new, click Create an account and complete registration.
  3. Forgot your password? Use Forgot password? on the login page to reset it by email.

One-click access from your enrollment email

After you enroll, you may receive a welcome email with a direct access link. Click that link to sign in automatically and go straight to your course — no separate login step required.

2. Finding your cohort course

  1. After signing in, open My Dashboard:
https://learn.allaboutultrasound.com/my-dashboard
  1. Select the My Content tab (if not already selected).
  2. Under Courses, find your cohort course card.
  3. Click Continue Learning to open the course player, or Overview for the course summary page.
If you already purchased the course, the course landing page may show a banner: “You already have access to this content.” Click Continue Learning there as well.

4. Live sessions

View your schedule

  1. Open your cohort hub (see Section 3).
  2. Click the Live Sessions tab.
  3. Review Upcoming sessions and Past Sessions.

Add sessions to your personal calendar

On an upcoming session card, click Add to Google Calendar, or open the Calendar tab and use Download ICS to import all sessions into Outlook, Apple Calendar, or another app.

Join a live session

  1. Return to Live Sessions shortly before class.
  2. When the join window opens, the session card shows Live Now (green).
  3. Click Join Live Session or Join Now.
  4. Your browser opens the meeting (Zoom, Teams, or the link provided by your instructor) in a new tab.
Join window: The Join Live Session button becomes available 15 minutes before the scheduled start time and stays available until the session ends. Before that window, the button may show “Join Live Session (opens 15 min before)”.

If a recording is attached to a past session

Some past session cards include Watch Recording, which opens the recording directly. Session replays are also collected in the Replays tab (Section 6).

5. Group discussions

Cohort Discussions are private to your assigned cohort group. They are separate from the public Community area on the platform.

How to participate

  1. Open your cohort hub.
  2. Click the Discussions tab.
  3. Read posts from classmates and instructors.
  4. Type in the composer (“Share something with your cohort…”).
  5. Optionally click + Add Image/Video to attach media.
  6. Click Post to publish your message.

Notifications

Use the Notifications On / Notifications Off toggle at the top of the Discussions tab to control email alerts for new posts in your group.

If discussions are not available

Message you seeWhat it means
“You are not assigned to a cohort group yet.” You are enrolled in the course but not placed in a group yet. Contact your program administrator.
“No discussions yet. Be the first to post!” Your group is ready — start the conversation.

6. Replay videos

Browse replays

  1. Open your cohort hub.
  2. Click the Replays tab.
  3. Browse recordings in grid or list view.
  4. Each card shows the title, linked session name, date, and duration.

Watch a replay

  1. Click any recording card.
  2. The replay player page opens with the video, title, and session details.
  3. Your watch progress is saved automatically.
  4. Click Back to Replays to return to the full list.
If the Replays tab is empty, your instructor has not published recordings yet. Check back after live sessions — replays appear here once uploaded.

Common issues

ProblemSolution
Cannot see my cohort Sign in at learn.allaboutultrasound.com. Confirm you are enrolled and open My Cohort from the course player or overview.
Join button is disabled Wait until 15 minutes before the session start time. Add the session to your calendar so you do not miss the window.
No discussions tab content Ask your administrator to confirm you are assigned to the correct cohort group.
No replays listed Recordings are added by your instructor after each session. Check the Replays tab again later.
Magic link expired Request a new link from the login page, or sign in with your password.

Need help?

Contact your course administrator or program coordinator with your registered email address and cohort group name. For login issues, use Forgot password? on the sign-in page first.
